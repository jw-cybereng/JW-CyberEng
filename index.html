Why is English essential in cybersecurity?

Most tools, documentation, and global teams communicate in English. If you can read and say the terms correctly, you move faster and make fewer mistakes.

GLOBAL COMMUNICATION

Around 95% of technical documentation, security tools, and international certifications (CEH, CISSP, CompTIA) are in English.

TECHNICAL TOOLS

Kali Linux, Wireshark, Metasploit, OWASP, and most frameworks use terminology primarily in English.

CAREER OPPORTUNITIES

Technical English unlocks international jobs, remote work, and access to global cybersecurity communities.

          Technical Reading (Cybersecurity)

          Reading 1: What is cybersecurity?

          Level: Intermediate • Words: ~230

          Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks. These attacks are usually aimed at accessing, changing, or destroying sensitive information; extorting money from users; or interrupting normal business operations.

          Implementing effective cybersecurity measures is challenging because there are more connected devices than ever before, and attackers keep becoming more innovative. A successful approach often uses defense-in-depth: multiple security layers so that if one control fails, others still protect the asset.

          In an organization, people, processes, and technology must complement one another. Key threats include malware, ransomware, phishing, social engineering, and advanced persistent threats (APTs). Understanding these threats is the first step toward a resilient security posture.

            Reading 2: How encryption works

            Level: Upper-intermediate • Words: ~210

            Encryption is the process of converting readable data, known as plaintext, into an encoded format called ciphertext. This transformation is done using an algorithm and an encryption key.

            There are two main types of encryption: symmetric and asymmetric. In symmetric encryption, the same key is used to encrypt and decrypt data. This method is fast but requires a secure way to share the secret key.

            Asymmetric encryption (public-key cryptography) uses two related keys: a public key for encryption and a private key for decryption. When you browse the web using HTTPS, TLS helps establish an encrypted connection with the server to protect your data from interception.
